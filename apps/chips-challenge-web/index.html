Summary

Chip’s Challenge was the first game I ever truly got addicted to, played endlessly on my dad’s old Windows 95 PC. Something about its puzzles, its rhythm, and its strange little world just clicked with 8-year-old me in a way no other game had. Rebuilding it now feels like reconnecting with the spark that first pulled me into software.
